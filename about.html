Heritage

Cali, Colombia — where it all started

My name is Valeria Cortes-Mora, and I'm originally from Cali, Colombia. My native language is Spanish, and my entire family is of Latin descent. That upbringing shaped how I see the world — as a set of connected places, cultures, and landscapes, not just points on a map.

I moved to the United States at 16 and have spent the years since studying, traveling, and building a career around geography — the field that first gave language to the things I noticed growing up between countries.

Currently

Studying Geography at UNM

I'm an undergraduate student at the University of New Mexico, majoring in Geography and minoring in Earth and Planetary Science. My coursework and research center on remote sensing, GIS, and the human impact on land — from cities to rainforests.

I'm a geography student who loves learning about different cultures and travel, which is really just the personal version of the same questions I study professionally.
