Portfolio

Research & projects.

My work sits at the intersection of remote sensing, geospatial AI, and environmental policy — using GIS to understand how people use and change land, in New Mexico and beyond.

Interests

What I study

Remote Sensing

Using satellite and aerial imagery to track land cover change over time.

Urbanization

Studying how cities grow and how that growth reshapes surrounding land.

Sustainability

Looking at resource use patterns that support — or undermine — long-term ecological health.

Forest Degradation

Mapping the pace and drivers of forest loss, especially in the Amazon basin.

Geospatial AI

Applying machine learning to geographic data for faster, more accurate land analysis.

Big Data Analysis

Working with large geospatial datasets to find patterns that aren't visible at a glance.

Water Resources

Examining water availability and management as a lens on climate impact.

Featured Work

Projects

GIS · New Mexico

Healthcare access analysis

An ongoing GIS project mapping the distribution of hospitals and urgent care facilities across New Mexico, examining where access gaps exist and who they affect.

Remote Sensing · Amazon Basin

Deforestation in Brazil & Colombia

Research into deforestation patterns in the Amazon Rainforest, with particular emphasis on the role of agricultural expansion and mining activity.

Web Application

Interactive Amazon deforestation map

A web application built around an interactive choropleth map of the Amazon Rainforest in Brazil, illustrating deforestation rates from 2000 to 2022. It's part of a broader set of visuals aimed at making this data legible to a non-technical audience.
