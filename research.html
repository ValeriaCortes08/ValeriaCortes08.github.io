Projects

Research · ArcGIS StoryMap · Python · JavaScript

Spatial-temporal analysis of Amazon rainforests

An ArcGIS StoryMap of the Amazon area in Colombia, built on big earth observation data. I used Python to run the spatial analysis and built an interactive JavaScript web map with time-series layers visualizing deforestation and socio-economic trends. Mentored by Dr. Liping Yang, Director of the UNM GeoAIR Lab (October 2023 – December 2024).

Earned the UNM ASSURE award and was presented at the Undergraduate Research Opportunity Conference (UROC).

GIS · Remote Sensing

Lead Service Line Inventory

Georeferenced and analyzed Landsat satellite imagery from Earth Explorer for a project on the San Carlos Apache Reservation, Arizona — determining whether homes were built before or after 1970 to help assess lead exposure risk. Completed as part of my work with the Southwest Environmental Finance Center.

Applied GIS · Field Work

Water & wastewater infrastructure mapping

Ongoing GIS mapping and digitization of drinking water and wastewater systems across New Mexico and other states, including supervised fieldwork and data collection with ArcGIS and Field Maps.
